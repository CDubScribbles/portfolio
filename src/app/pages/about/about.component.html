About me

I grew up in a lake town in northeast Georgia and spent most of my formative years in Southern California. Lake water and sunsets are still my favorite color palette, which is why this site looks the way it does.

My path here is not a straight line. I earned an MFA in Acting at USC, worked in marketing and publishing, and spent about a decade in upscale Los Angeles hospitality before moving into eCommerce and, now, web development. Reading a room in real time turned out to be excellent training for building software people actually enjoy using.

Today I build accessible, well-tested web apps with Angular and TypeScript, and I'm finishing a B.S. in Web Development at Bellevue University (expected 2026).

Off the clock, I love cooking and science fiction, and cyberpunk is a standing obsession: the game, Neuromancer, all of it.
